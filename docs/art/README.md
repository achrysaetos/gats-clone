# Art direction

`gold-standard.webp` is the art-direction reference for Skirmish. When the game's look and this image disagree, the image wins, within what the gameplay needs to stay readable.

What it sets:

- A near-white concrete floor with a faint grid and light stains.
- Cover drawn top-down: the top face is the collision rect, with a fine material texture, a thin dark outline, a thin dark lip on the bottom and right, and a long soft shadow down and to the right.
- Flat, softly shaded bodies with a thin outline and a short black gun.
- A dashed white trail behind moving bodies, and rounds as short glowing dashes.
- Damage left on cover as hairline cracks and grey chips.
- A minimal HUD in small dark grey panels.
