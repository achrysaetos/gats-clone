The instrument samples in this folder (<instrument>-<midi note>.mp3) are single notes cut from the Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont,
as rendered to MP3 by the gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts project (https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts, FluidR3_GM), then trimmed,
faded, levelled and re-encoded by scripts/build-music-samples.ts. They are used as the sampled voices of the in-game music
(src/client/musicsamples.ts).
