# Zombies

Zombies is co-op base defense for a private squad of up to four, with bots in the empty seats. The squad holds the core, presented as the Bastion with 50 survivors inside, at the center of the Outpost map through a 40-second day of building walls and turrets and repairing them, then a night whose horde walks in from the edges the night table names. A night ends once its whole horde has spawned and died. Ten nights grow harder, night 5 brings a Colossus and night 10, the Tide, brings everything from every side; holding through the Tide wins the run, and the core's fall loses it. Either ends the run with a report; 20 seconds later a fresh run starts. A player whose health runs out goes down, can be revived by a squadmate holding use for 3 seconds, and otherwise bleeds out after 25 seconds and returns at dawn. A human who joins during the night sits out until dawn the same way. Walkers and plated turn on a living squad player within 120px in plain sight, runners within 360px; brutes, bloaters and the Colossus ignore the squad and march on the core, which shrugs off half of each bite and can be repaired. Plating takes 10 off every bullet, down to 1, unless the round pierces; blasts get through whole. A bloater bursts where it dies, hurting squad, horde and every building within 110px. Sentries, cannons, scatters and mortars fire real rounds at the horde on ammo bought from the shared scrap bank, and the Bastion's survivors fire from its walls. The browser client starts and joins squads from the menu, draws the horde, the core, the walls and the turrets, and has a build mode, a run HUD, callouts, sounds and the report.

## Sub-features

- `zom-squad-room` opens with `POST /api/squads`, which answers `{ room: 'z-' + 6 base-32 characters }`. `GET /api/servers` never lists it. One address may open `squadsPerMin` (6) a minute (429 after), at most `squadRooms` (20) run at once (503 after), and a room with no humans for `squadIdleMs` (30s) closes and its code stops answering the upgrade. That 30s is wall-clock time. `LIMITS` in `src/server/limits.ts` holds all three.
- `zom-standstill` freezes a squad room while no human is joined: the day clock, the night, bleed-outs and the restart countdown all wait. A squad nobody has joined yet sits on day 1 with 40s left, and a human who rejoins within the 30s idle window picks up where the room stopped.
- `zom-seats` keeps four players: bots fill the seats humans leave, a joining human takes a bot's seat, and a fifth human gets `{t:'error', message:'Room full'}`. Everyone is on team `red`, so friendly fire never applies; in a run only zombie bites hurt the squad, never its own blasts.
- `zom-run` is the state machine in `src/shared/sim/run.ts`: `day` (until `phaseEndsAt`, or sooner once every living human sent `{t:'ready'}`; `ready` lists their ids, bots count as ready but a squad of bots alone waits out the day), `night` (`phaseEndsAt` null; `waveLeft` counts zombies alive or to come), `over` (`phaseEndsAt` is the restart time and `report` holds night reached, `won` (true after the Tide's dawn), `survivors`, `durationMs`, a row of kills, revives and buildings built for every player in the squad when the core fell and for anyone who left after scoring one of those, and `turretKills` by turret kind). The snapshot's `run` field carries it with `scrap`, `core` (`x`, `y`, `hp`, `maxHp`), `aliveZombies`, `survivors` and `lost` (tonight's, or last night's by day). `NIGHTS` in `src/shared/defs.ts` is the night table: one row per night with each kind's count for four bots (a human counts one and a half, every listed kind sends at least one), the sides it comes from (`north`, `east`, `south`, `west`), and a `name` on the boss nights. The horde spawns as packs (`ZOMBIES[kind].pack`) from those sides only. Survivors are `ceil(core hp / coreHp × 50)` but never rise again, so mending the core shelters the rest without raising anyone; each one left pays 2 scrap at dawn.
- `zom-horde` sends each player the zombies in view as tuples `[id, kindIndex, x, y, hpTenths]` in `snap.zombies` (`kindIndex` into `ZOMBIE_KINDS`: 0 walker, 1 brute, 2 runner, 3 plated, 4 bloater, 5 colossus). A zombie death ships a `zkill` event whose `by` is the player whose own shot, blade or blast killed it, null for a turret's kill; each player sees `dmg` events (kind `zombie`) only for their own hits, one per zombie per tick. A blast or a turret round sends no per-zombie `dmg`.
- `zom-walls` takes `{t:'build', kind, cx, cy}` (`kind` one of `BUILDING_KINDS`: `wall`, `sentry`, `cannon`, `scatter`, `mortar`) and `{t:'demolish', cx, cy}` on the 50px grid (`ZOM.cell`). `buildRefusal()` in `src/shared/sim/build.ts` holds the rules, and both the server and the client's build preview call it. A cell with a building on it reads `taken` before anything else, so it can come down with someone pressed against it. `snap.buildings` lists `{kind, cx, cy, hp}` (hp in tenths) plus `ammo` (tenths of a full load, 0 once it cannot fire) on a turret, and is sticky: the wire omits it while unchanged. Buildings block bodies but not the squad's bullets or grenades. Costs, health and every turret number live in `BUILDINGS` in `src/shared/defs.ts`.
- `zom-turrets` is `tickTurrets()` in `src/shared/sim/turrets.ts`. A loaded turret whose gun has cooled fires one real bullet (`Bullet.turret` set, owner its builder) at the nearest zombie in range of the kind it `prefers` (sentry walkers, cannon brutes, scatter runners, mortar plated), else the nearest of any kind, skipping any that a map wall or crate hides unless it lobs. Squad buildings never block its view. A scatter fires `pellets` (7) rounds a shot over 260px. A mortar's shell flies over everything to where its target stood and bursts there (`lobbed`, a 120px blast that ignores plating); its `turret` event carries `reach`. The Bastion fires `BASTION_GUN` rounds from the core's center at zombies within 220px, once per 300ms with all 50 survivors and slower as they fall; they are plain rounds on `snap.bullets` with no owner. Each shot spends one ammo and ships `{e:'turret', kind, x, y, angle}` (cell center, angle to 0.01 rad). Turret rounds stay off `snap.bullets`; the client flies each from its event to the first zombie on its line. A turret's kill pays the bank, scores for its builder while they are in the room, and adds to `run.turretKills[kind][zombieKind]`, never to anyone's `kills`.
- `zom-downed` shows a downed player in `players` with `downed: { revive (0..1), bleedOutAt (server time) }`; `alive` is false. `life` events report `downed`, `revived` (with `by`) and `bledOut`. A bled-out player's `self.respawnIn` stays 0 and the `respawn` message does nothing until dawn brings them back. A human whose `join` lands during the night gets the same dead state, so leaving and rejoining never skips going down.
- `zom-use` is the `use` flag on `InputState`, bound to E: held within 70px of a downed squadmate it revives. Otherwise it serves the nearest damaged building, worn core or turret short of ammo within 250px (of the cell center or the core's center) for scrap, day or night: 1 per 20 hp on a building, 1 per 5 hp on the core, and `scrapPerRound` per round reloaded, a full load in `ZOM.refillMs` (2.5s). A worn turret is repaired before it is reloaded. Squad bots run errands, first match wins: revive a downed squadmate (a human before a bot, horde or not); while no zombie in sight is within 300px, mend a worn building or reload a turret within 550px of the core (a turret only while the bank has scrap); by day in a squad with no other human, put up the next building of `BASTION_PLAN` in `src/server/bot/siege.ts` once the bank can pay, from a spot two cells to its side; then mend the core while the bank has scrap beyond that building's cost; else hold a post 320px out from the core on a bearing set by the bot's id, or just inside the first squad wall on that bearing. A zombie within 140px makes a posted bot back off or sidestep while staying inside 550px. They shoot the nearest zombie in sight throughout, and head for the core's nearer corner when the core stands between them and where they are going.
- `zom-menu` is the Zombies block under the room list (`#squad`). `#squad-start` posts `/api/squads` and joins at once, and puts `?squad=<code>` in the address bar. A page opened with `?squad=<code>` lists the squad as `#squad-room` (selected), shows `#squad-link` with `#squad-copy`, and scrolls to it once the rooms load. A malformed code shows `That invite link is broken…` in `#menu-status` and drops the code from the address bar; a well-formed code whose room is gone shows `Squad <code> has closed. Start a new one.` and drops the code from the address bar. In game, `#squad-chip` names the squad with a copy button.
- `zom-world` draws walkers (small green, two arms), brutes (large purple, shoulder pads, red eyes, an hp bar), runners (smallest, sand), plated (steel grey with a thick ink rim and yellow eyes), bloaters (large orange) and the Colossus (huge dark red, shoulder pads, yellow eyes, an hp bar) as flat discs with an ink rim, with batched arms and eyes per kind, with a white flash on your own hits and a green splat on each `zkill`. The core is a tall dark block with a cyan crystal that turns red at 35% health or less, an hp ring and a white flash when it loses health. Walls are brick-coursed blocks that darken and crack by hp and flash when bitten. Every building stands in the same fixed tilt as the map, top face on its cell and shadow down and to the right. A turret is a low concrete pad under a flat dome (grey for a sentry, brown for a cannon, teal-grey for a scatter, olive for a mortar) with a barrel (twin thin barrels on a sentry, one thick one on a cannon, a flared mouth on a scatter, a short fat tube on a mortar) and an accent dot on the dome (yellow on a sentry, red on a cannon, teal on a scatter, violet on a mortar), that eases toward each shot's angle and recoils, and faces away from the core until it first fires. It has a muzzle flash, an ammo bar along its bottom edge that blinks red when empty, and the walls' cracks and bite flash. Downed squadmates lie flat and squashed with a white cross, a revive ring and a bleed-out clock. Squadmates wear their own colors, since the squad shares one team. At night the floor, walls, buildings, the core and cracks darken toward a cool blue over 1.5s while rounds, bodies and effects drawn after the wash stay bright, and the HUD's text drawn straight on the world turns light.
- `zom-build` is build mode: B toggles it by day (at night the chat says `You build by day.`), and nightfall or going down turns it off. 1 to 5 pick wall, sentry, cannon, scatter and mortar (`buildKindForKey`), and so does a click on a kind chip in the picker row above the build bar; the picked chip is gold. While build mode is on, the number keys pick kinds, never perks, and the perk dock waits. A ghost cell of the picked kind follows the cursor: green with `Sentry · 70 scrap`, orange over a squad building with `Right click to take down the cannon · +90`, red with the refusal otherwise (`Cannon needs 180 scrap` when the bank is short). Dashed rings show the build radius around the core and your reach. Left click builds, right click takes down; neither fires. The build bar at the bottom lists the clicks and B.
- `zom-hud` is the score pill's phase (`DAY 2` with the clock to night, `NIGHT 3` with zombies left, `FALLEN`), the phase line under it while Tab is held (`Outpost · Day 2 · night in 0:31`, `Night 3 · 12 left`, `Core fell · next run in 0:14`), a small panel with the scrap bank, a core health bar and the survivors left (red while the core alert shows), by day a gold `Tonight · Runners and walkers from the east` line under it, `CORE UNDER ATTACK` for 1.5s after the core loses health with an edge arrow when the core is off screen, the minimap's core, walls (sand), sentries (gold), cannons (orange) and zombie dots, `Hold E to revive <name>`, `Hold E to repair the wall` (or `sentry`, `cannon`, `core`), `Hold E to reload the sentry` (or `cannon`), with every E hint but revive hidden while the bank is empty, a `B build walls and turrets` and `N bring the night now` (or `ready for night · 1/2`, then `ready · 1/2 · N to wait`) hint bar by day outside build mode, the downed banner (`You're down` · `Crawl to a squadmate · 0:18` or `Being revived · 45%`), the `Squad kills` board, and kill-feed lines for downs, revives and bleed-outs. Callouts: `Night falls in 10` with tonight's forecast, `Night N` (or the night's name, `The Colossus`, `The Tide`) with the horde size and its sides, `Dawn` with the survivors lost and left and the scrap gained, then `Tonight` with the next forecast, `Back on your feet`. A bled-out player gets the death card titled `You bled out` with `Back at dawn · N zombies left tonight` (just `Back at dawn` once the night is over) and no respawn button or loadout; a night joiner gets the same card titled `The night is under way`. `#report` shows the run report (`The Bastion fell on night N`, or in gold `The Bastion held. N survivors saw the morning.` after the Tide, with the score pill reading `HELD`, the run length, a table sorted by kills, then revives, then builds, and `Next run in Ns`), with a `Turret kills · Sentry 41 · Cannon 7` line when turrets killed anything, and while it does the canvas draws no callouts, core alert, downed banner or key hints, and the perk dock stays shut. In a run the perk dock sits above the bottom key-hint row.
- `zom-horde-path` walks the horde on a flow field out from the core. Map cover is impassable and a squad wall costs 40 cells of walking, so zombies take any open way round and chew through a wall only when the core is walled in. A building bitten to nothing ships a `boom` event and drops.
- `zom-sounds` adds synth cues in `src/client/sfx.ts`: `bite`, `splat`, `wallHit`, `wallUp`, `wallDown`, `coreHit` (once per 100 core hp), `horn`, `chime`, `downed`, `revived`, and `turret:sentry` and `turret:cannon` (once per kind per snapshot). M mutes them with everything else.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the menu, scroll to Zombies, press Start a squad. Copy the invite link from the menu or the in-game chip and send it; a friend opening it lands on the menu with the squad selected and presses Play.
- By day read the gold `Tonight` line for what comes and from where, and press N when ready for it. Press B, press 1 to 5 (or click a chip) to pick a wall, sentry, cannon, scatter or mortar, point at a cell near the core and click. Hold E by a worn building or the worn core to repair it, by a turret short of ammo to reload it, or by a downed squadmate to revive them.

## Gotchas

- `doctor.sh` checks only the three public rooms. A squad exists only after a `POST /api/squads`.
- A squad with no human closes after 30s, bots and all, so open it and join within that window.
- `zombies.ts` needs no browser and launches none.
- The golden replay excludes zombies runs; `test/zombies-determinism.test.ts` guards their determinism instead.
- Bots revive fast, so the bled-out screen needs the fragile scratch copy and some luck; a run that logs `note revived before bleeding out` proves the downed UI but not the bled-out card.
- Night 1's forecast side is fixed by the night table (north), so `the night's zombies walk in only from the sides it forecast` checks one side; a scratch row with two sides checks more.
- Bots build only when no other human is in the squad, so a driven browser's scrap is its own to spend; a bot squad's build plan shows only in the bench or a room with no human.
- The core alert needs zombies to reach the core. On the stock night 1 a squad often stops them first, so `zom-core-alert.png` may be missing from the default run; the fragile copy always reaches it.
- Build mode has no touch control yet; on a phone B is out of reach.
- Stock day 1 affords one sentry after the build step's wall and never a cannon; the cannon path needs the more-scrap scratch copy.
- A bot near a sentry reloads it far faster than it fires, so its ammo bar may sit at 10/10 all night. The turret unit tests pin the tenths, and the browser run logs how low each bar went.
