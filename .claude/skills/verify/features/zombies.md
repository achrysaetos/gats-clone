# Zombies

Zombies is co-op base defense for a private squad of up to four, with bots in the empty seats. The squad holds a core at the center of the Outpost map through a 40-second day of building and repairing walls, then a night whose wave walks in from the four edges. A night ends once its whole wave has spawned and died. Waves grow every night until the core falls, which ends the run with a report; 20 seconds later a fresh run starts. A player whose health runs out goes down, can be revived by a squadmate holding use for 3 seconds, and otherwise bleeds out after 25 seconds and returns at dawn. A human who joins during the night sits out until dawn the same way. Walkers chase squad players in sight; brutes ignore the squad and march on the core, which shrugs off 60% of each bite and can be repaired. The browser client starts and joins squads from the menu, draws the horde, the core and the walls, and has a build mode, a run HUD, callouts, sounds and the report.

## Sub-features

- `zom-squad-room` opens with `POST /api/squads`, which answers `{ room: 'z-' + 6 base-32 characters }`. `GET /api/servers` never lists it. One address may open `squadsPerMin` (6) a minute (429 after), at most `squadRooms` (20) run at once (503 after), and a room with no humans for `squadIdleMs` (30s) closes and its code stops answering the upgrade. `LIMITS` in `src/server/limits.ts` holds all three.
- `zom-seats` keeps four players: bots fill the seats humans leave, a joining human takes a bot's seat, and a fifth human gets `{t:'error', message:'Room full'}`. Everyone is on team `red`, so friendly fire never applies; in a run only zombie bites hurt the squad, never its own blasts.
- `zom-run` is the state machine in `src/shared/sim/run.ts`: `day` (until `phaseEndsAt`), `night` (`phaseEndsAt` null; `waveLeft` counts zombies alive or to come), `over` (`phaseEndsAt` is the restart time and `report` holds night reached, `durationMs` and a row of kills, revives and walls built for every player in the squad when the core fell). The snapshot's `run` field carries it with `scrap`, `core` (`x`, `y`, `hp`, `maxHp`) and `aliveZombies`.
- `zom-horde` sends each player the zombies in view as tuples `[id, kindIndex, x, y, hpTenths]` in `snap.zombies` (`kindIndex` into `ZOMBIE_KINDS`: 0 walker, 1 brute). A zombie death ships a `zkill` event; each player sees `dmg` events (kind `zombie`) only for their own hits, one per zombie per tick. A blast sends no per-zombie `dmg`, only its `boom`.
- `zom-walls` takes `{t:'build', cx, cy}` and `{t:'demolish', cx, cy}` on the 50px grid (`ZOM.cell`). `buildRefusal()` in `src/shared/sim/build.ts` holds the rules, and both the server and the client's build preview call it. `snap.buildings` lists `{kind, cx, cy, hp}` (hp in tenths) and is sticky: the wire omits it while unchanged. Walls block bodies but not the squad's bullets or grenades.
- `zom-downed` shows a downed player in `players` with `downed: { revive (0..1), bleedOutAt (server time) }`; `alive` is false. `life` events report `downed`, `revived` (with `by`) and `bledOut`. A bled-out player's `self.respawnIn` stays 0 and the `respawn` message does nothing until dawn brings them back. A human whose `join` lands during the night gets the same dead state, so leaving and rejoining never skips going down.
- `zom-use` is the `use` flag on `InputState`, bound to E: held within 70px of a downed squadmate it revives, otherwise it repairs the nearest damaged wall or the core within 250px (of the wall's cell center or the core's center) for scrap: 1 per 20 hp on a wall, 1 per 5 hp on the core, day or night. Squad bots do the same while no zombie is within 300px, and post just inside the first squad wall on their bearing from the core.
- `zom-menu` is the Zombies block under the room list (`#squad`). `#squad-start` posts `/api/squads` and joins at once, and puts `?squad=<code>` in the address bar. A page opened with `?squad=<code>` lists the squad as `#squad-room` (selected), shows `#squad-link` with `#squad-copy`, and scrolls to it once the rooms load. A malformed code shows `That invite link is broken…` in `#menu-status`; a well-formed code whose room is gone shows `Squad <code> has closed. Start a new one.` and drops the code from the address bar. In game, `#squad-chip` names the squad with a copy button.
- `zom-world` draws walkers (small green, two arms) and brutes (large purple, shoulder pads, red eyes, an hp bar) in a handful of batched paths per kind, with a white flash on your own hits and a green splat on each `zkill`. The core is a dark plate with a cyan crystal, an hp ring and a white flash when it loses health. Walls are brick tiles that darken and crack by hp and flash when bitten. Downed squadmates lie low and grey with a revive ring and a bleed-out clock. Squadmates wear their own colors, since the squad shares one team.
- `zom-build` is build mode: B toggles it by day (at night the chat says walls go up by day), and nightfall or going down turns it off. A ghost cell follows the cursor: green with `Wall · 20 scrap`, orange over your squad's wall with `Right click to take down · +10`, red with the refusal otherwise. Dashed rings show the build radius around the core and your reach. Left click builds, right click takes down; neither fires. A hint bar at the bottom lists the keys.
- `zom-hud` is the phase line under the level bar (`Outpost · Day 2 · night in 0:31`, `Night 3 · 12 left`, `Core fell · next run in 0:14`), a bar with the scrap bank and core health, `CORE UNDER ATTACK` for 1.5s after the core loses health with an edge arrow when the core is off screen, the minimap's core, walls and zombie dots, `Hold E to revive <name>`, `Hold E to repair the wall` or `Hold E to repair the core`, the downed banner (`You're down` · `Crawl to a squadmate · 0:18` or `Being revived · 45%`), the `Squad kills` board, and kill-feed lines for downs, revives and bleed-outs. Callouts: `Night falls in 10`, `Night N` with the wave size, `Dawn` with the core and scrap, `Back on your feet`. A bled-out player gets the death card titled `You bled out` with `Back at dawn` and no respawn button or loadout; a night joiner gets the same card titled `The night is under way`. `#report` shows the run report, and while it does the canvas draws no callouts, core alert, downed banner or key hints, and the perk dock stays shut. In a run the perk dock sits above the bottom key-hint row.
- `zom-sounds` adds synth cues in `src/client/sfx.ts`: `bite`, `splat`, `wallHit`, `wallUp`, `wallDown`, `coreHit` (once per 100 core hp), `horn`, `chime`, `downed` and `revived`. M mutes them with everything else.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the menu, scroll to Zombies, press Start a squad. Copy the invite link from the menu or the in-game chip and send it; a friend opening it lands on the menu with the squad selected and presses Play.
- By day press B, point at a cell near the core and click. Hold E by a worn wall or the worn core to repair it, or by a downed squadmate to revive them.

## Driving it with drive.ts

Preconditions:

- Doctor passes.

- **The client in a browser.** `node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/zombies-ui.ts "$RUN" [step ...]` drives headless Chrome (muted) through real input. Default steps: `menu badlink squad build night`. It logs `menu offers to start a zombies squad`, `a broken invite link says so on the menu`, `joining a squad that is gone says it has closed`, `Start a squad joins a zombies room`, `the squad stays off the public room list`, `the invite link is this page with only the squad code`, `a friend following the invite link lands in the same squad` (a `ws` client joining by the link's code), `reopening the invite link selects the squad on the menu`, `a left click puts the wall up on the server` and `the wall cost its scrap` (read from the page's own snapshots), `the ghost turns red with a reason over the core`, `a right click takes the wall down for half its cost`, `a warning callout comes ten seconds before night`, `night turns build mode off`, `the squad saw zombies in view` (four or more in one snapshot), `the driven player shot zombies through real input`, and `a Dawn callout sums up the night`. Callouts, the ghost and build mode come from the `?dev` hook `skirmishDev.zombies()`; `skirmishDev.toScreen(x, y)` maps world to screen for aiming. The fight aims along the line from the player to the nearest zombie, pulled in to a point over the canvas inside a box clear of the HUD panels, and lets go and presses again when the ammo has not moved for a second. Screenshots: `zom-menu`, `zom-badlink`, `zom-squad-joined`, `zom-menu-squad`, `zom-ghost-valid`, `zom-wall-built`, `zom-ghost-invalid`, `zom-dusk-warning`, `zom-night-callout`, `zom-night`, `zom-core-alert` (only if the core is bitten), `zom-dawn`. Log: `$RUN/evidence/zombies-ui.log`. About 2 minutes.
- **Downed, bled out and the report.** Launch a scratch copy, never by editing the repo: `rsync -a --exclude node_modules --exclude .git <repo>/ "$SCR/repo/"`, `ln -s <repo>/node_modules "$SCR/repo/node_modules"`, then in `$SCR/repo/src/shared/defs.ts` set the walker's `damage` to 60, `ZOM.coreHp` to 900, `ZOM.bleedOutMs` to 10_000 and `ZOM.dayMs` to 15_000. Run `$SCR/repo/.claude/skills/verify/scripts/launch.sh "$SCR/run"` and `node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/zombies-ui.ts "$SCR/run" squad downed report`. It walks into the horde until `the driven player goes down to a bite`, checks `a downed player stays in play, not on the death screen`, then either `it says back at dawn instead of offering a respawn` or `note revived before bleeding out` (a bot got there first), and plays on until `the core falls` and `the report shows the night reached and a row per player`. Screenshots: `zom-downed`, `zom-bled-out`, `zom-core-alert`, `zom-report`.
- **Frame cost with a full horde.** Scratch copy as above with the walker at `hp: 4000, damage: 0.5`, the brute at `hp: 20000, damage: 1, firstNight: 1`, `coreHp: 400000`, `dayMs: 4_000`, `spawnGapMs` returning 20 and `waveSize` returning 600. Then `SQUAD=1 node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/frametime.ts "$SCR/run" 20` (add `SOFTWARE=1` for the rastered cost). It logs zombies in view per snapshot beside the frame cost.
- **First night over `ws`.** `node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/zombies.ts "$RUN" [seconds]` (default 150) opens a squad, joins as one human with an LMG and logs `welcome says mode ZOM`, `the run opens on day 1`, `3 bots fill the squad beside one human`, `a build message put a wall on cell <cx>,<cy>`, `the wall cost 20 scrap`, then `night 1 over after <s>s: …` and `dawn of night 2 arrived`. Log: `$RUN/evidence/zombies.log`. No browser.

## Gotchas

- `doctor.sh` checks only the three public rooms. A squad exists only after a `POST /api/squads`.
- A squad with no human closes after 30s, bots and all, so open it and join within that window.
- `zombies.ts` needs no browser and launches none.
- The golden replay excludes zombies runs; `test/zombies-determinism.test.ts` guards their determinism instead.
- Bots revive fast, so the bled-out screen needs the fragile scratch copy and some luck; a run that logs `note revived before bleeding out` proves the downed UI but not the bled-out card.
- The core alert needs zombies to reach the core. On the stock night 1 a squad often stops them first, so `zom-core-alert.png` may be missing from the default run; the fragile copy always reaches it.
- Build mode has no touch control yet; on a phone B is out of reach.
