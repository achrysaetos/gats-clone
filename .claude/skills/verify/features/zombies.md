# Zombies

Zombies is co-op base defense for a private squad of up to four, with bots in the empty seats. The squad holds a core at the center of the Outpost map through a 40-second day of building walls and turrets and repairing them, then a night whose wave walks in from the four edges. A night ends once its whole wave has spawned and died. Waves grow every night until the core falls, which ends the run with a report; 20 seconds later a fresh run starts. A player whose health runs out goes down, can be revived by a squadmate holding use for 3 seconds, and otherwise bleeds out after 25 seconds and returns at dawn. A human who joins during the night sits out until dawn the same way. Walkers chase squad players in sight; brutes ignore the squad and march on the core, which shrugs off 60% of each bite and can be repaired. Sentries and cannons fire real rounds at the horde on ammo bought from the shared scrap bank. The browser client starts and joins squads from the menu, draws the horde, the core, the walls and the turrets, and has a build mode, a run HUD, callouts, sounds and the report.

## Sub-features

- `zom-squad-room` opens with `POST /api/squads`, which answers `{ room: 'z-' + 6 base-32 characters }`. `GET /api/servers` never lists it. One address may open `squadsPerMin` (6) a minute (429 after), at most `squadRooms` (20) run at once (503 after), and a room with no humans for `squadIdleMs` (30s) closes and its code stops answering the upgrade. `LIMITS` in `src/server/limits.ts` holds all three.
- `zom-seats` keeps four players: bots fill the seats humans leave, a joining human takes a bot's seat, and a fifth human gets `{t:'error', message:'Room full'}`. Everyone is on team `red`, so friendly fire never applies; in a run only zombie bites hurt the squad, never its own blasts.
- `zom-run` is the state machine in `src/shared/sim/run.ts`: `day` (until `phaseEndsAt`), `night` (`phaseEndsAt` null; `waveLeft` counts zombies alive or to come), `over` (`phaseEndsAt` is the restart time and `report` holds night reached, `durationMs`, a row of kills, revives and buildings built for every player in the squad when the core fell, and `turretKills` by turret kind). The snapshot's `run` field carries it with `scrap`, `core` (`x`, `y`, `hp`, `maxHp`) and `aliveZombies`.
- `zom-horde` sends each player the zombies in view as tuples `[id, kindIndex, x, y, hpTenths]` in `snap.zombies` (`kindIndex` into `ZOMBIE_KINDS`: 0 walker, 1 brute). A zombie death ships a `zkill` event whose `by` is the player whose own shot, blade or blast killed it, null for a turret's kill; each player sees `dmg` events (kind `zombie`) only for their own hits, one per zombie per tick. A blast or a turret round sends no per-zombie `dmg`.
- `zom-walls` takes `{t:'build', kind, cx, cy}` (`kind` one of `BUILDING_KINDS`: `wall`, `sentry`, `cannon`) and `{t:'demolish', cx, cy}` on the 50px grid (`ZOM.cell`). `buildRefusal()` in `src/shared/sim/build.ts` holds the rules, and both the server and the client's build preview call it. A cell with a building on it reads `taken` before anything else, so it can come down with someone pressed against it. `snap.buildings` lists `{kind, cx, cy, hp}` (hp in tenths) plus `ammo` (tenths of a full load, 0 once it cannot fire) on a turret, and is sticky: the wire omits it while unchanged. Buildings block bodies but not the squad's bullets or grenades. Costs, health and every turret number live in `BUILDINGS` in `src/shared/defs.ts`.
- `zom-turrets` is `tickTurrets()` in `src/shared/sim/turrets.ts`. A loaded turret whose gun has cooled fires one real bullet (`Bullet.turret` set, owner its builder) at the nearest zombie in range of the kind it `prefers` (sentry walkers, cannon brutes), else the nearest of any kind, skipping any that a map wall or crate hides. Squad buildings never block its view. A round's damage depends on the kind it hits (`damage.walker`, `damage.brute`). Each shot spends one ammo and ships `{e:'turret', kind, x, y, angle}` (cell center, angle to 0.01 rad). Turret rounds stay off `snap.bullets`; the client flies each from its event to the first zombie on its line. A turret's kill pays the bank, scores for its builder while they are in the room, and adds to `run.turretKills[kind][zombieKind]`, never to anyone's `kills`.
- `zom-downed` shows a downed player in `players` with `downed: { revive (0..1), bleedOutAt (server time) }`; `alive` is false. `life` events report `downed`, `revived` (with `by`) and `bledOut`. A bled-out player's `self.respawnIn` stays 0 and the `respawn` message does nothing until dawn brings them back. A human whose `join` lands during the night gets the same dead state, so leaving and rejoining never skips going down.
- `zom-use` is the `use` flag on `InputState`, bound to E: held within 70px of a downed squadmate it revives. Otherwise it serves the nearest damaged building, worn core or turret short of ammo within 250px (of the cell center or the core's center) for scrap, day or night: 1 per 20 hp on a building, 1 per 5 hp on the core, and `scrapPerRound` per round reloaded, a full load in `ZOM.refillMs` (2.5s). A worn turret is repaired before it is reloaded. Squad bots do the same while no zombie is within 300px (a turret only while the bank has scrap), and post just inside the first squad wall on their bearing from the core.
- `zom-menu` is the Zombies block under the room list (`#squad`). `#squad-start` posts `/api/squads` and joins at once, and puts `?squad=<code>` in the address bar. A page opened with `?squad=<code>` lists the squad as `#squad-room` (selected), shows `#squad-link` with `#squad-copy`, and scrolls to it once the rooms load. A malformed code shows `That invite link is broken…` in `#menu-status`; a well-formed code whose room is gone shows `Squad <code> has closed. Start a new one.` and drops the code from the address bar. In game, `#squad-chip` names the squad with a copy button.
- `zom-world` draws walkers (small green, two arms) and brutes (large purple, shoulder pads, red eyes, an hp bar) in a handful of batched paths per kind, with a white flash on your own hits and a green splat on each `zkill`. The core is a dark plate with a cyan crystal, an hp ring and a white flash when it loses health. Walls are brick tiles that darken and crack by hp and flash when bitten. A turret is a plate (grey for a sentry, brown for a cannon) with a barrel (twin thin barrels on a sentry, one thick one on a cannon) that eases toward each shot's angle and recoils, and faces away from the core until it first fires. It has a muzzle flash, an ammo bar along its bottom edge that blinks red when empty, and the walls' cracks and bite flash. Downed squadmates lie low and grey with a revive ring and a bleed-out clock. Squadmates wear their own colors, since the squad shares one team.
- `zom-build` is build mode: B toggles it by day (at night the chat says `You build by day.`), and nightfall or going down turns it off. 1, 2 and 3 pick wall, sentry and cannon (`buildKindForKey`), and so does a click on a kind chip in the picker row above the build bar; the picked chip is gold. While build mode is on, the number keys pick kinds, never perks, and the perk dock waits. A ghost cell of the picked kind follows the cursor: green with `Sentry · 70 scrap`, orange over a squad building with `Right click to take down the cannon · +90`, red with the refusal otherwise (`Cannon needs 180 scrap` when the bank is short). Dashed rings show the build radius around the core and your reach. Left click builds, right click takes down; neither fires. The build bar at the bottom lists the clicks and B.
- `zom-hud` is the phase line under the level bar (`Outpost · Day 2 · night in 0:31`, `Night 3 · 12 left`, `Core fell · next run in 0:14`), a bar with the scrap bank and core health, `CORE UNDER ATTACK` for 1.5s after the core loses health with an edge arrow when the core is off screen, the minimap's core, walls (sand), sentries (gold), cannons (orange) and zombie dots, `Hold E to revive <name>`, `Hold E to repair the wall` (or `sentry`, `cannon`, `core`), `Hold E to reload the sentry` (or `cannon`), the downed banner (`You're down` · `Crawl to a squadmate · 0:18` or `Being revived · 45%`), the `Squad kills` board, and kill-feed lines for downs, revives and bleed-outs. Callouts: `Night falls in 10`, `Night N` with the wave size, `Dawn` with the core and scrap, `Back on your feet`. A bled-out player gets the death card titled `You bled out` with `Back at dawn` and no respawn button or loadout; a night joiner gets the same card titled `The night is under way`. `#report` shows the run report, with a `Turret kills · Sentry 41 · Cannon 7` line when turrets killed anything, and while it does the canvas draws no callouts, core alert, downed banner or key hints, and the perk dock stays shut. In a run the perk dock sits above the bottom key-hint row.
- `zom-sounds` adds synth cues in `src/client/sfx.ts`: `bite`, `splat`, `wallHit`, `wallUp`, `wallDown`, `coreHit` (once per 100 core hp), `horn`, `chime`, `downed`, `revived`, and `turret:sentry` and `turret:cannon` (once per kind per snapshot). M mutes them with everything else.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the menu, scroll to Zombies, press Start a squad. Copy the invite link from the menu or the in-game chip and send it; a friend opening it lands on the menu with the squad selected and presses Play.
- By day press B, press 1, 2 or 3 (or click a chip) to pick a wall, sentry or cannon, point at a cell near the core and click. Hold E by a worn building or the worn core to repair it, by a turret short of ammo to reload it, or by a downed squadmate to revive them.

Preconditions:

- Doctor passes.

- **The client in a browser.** `node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/zombies-ui.ts "$RUN" [step ...]` drives headless Chrome (muted) through real input. Default steps: `menu badlink squad build turrets night`. It logs `menu offers to start a zombies squad`, `a broken invite link says so on the menu`, `joining a squad that is gone says it has closed`, `Start a squad joins a zombies room`, `the squad stays off the public room list`, `the invite link is this page with only the squad code`, `a friend following the invite link lands in the same squad` (a `ws` client joining by the link's code), `reopening the invite link selects the squad on the menu`, `a left click puts the wall up on the server` and `the wall cost its scrap` (read from the page's own snapshots), `the ghost turns red with a reason over the core`, `a right click takes the wall down for half its cost`, `2 picks the sentry in build mode`, `the ghost shows a sentry over a buildable cell`, `a left click puts the sentry up on the server, fully loaded`, `the sentry cost its scrap`, `a click on the hint bar's cannon chip picks the cannon`, then on stock scrap (the build step leaves exactly 70) `the ghost says a cannon needs 180 scrap when the bank is short`, `a warning callout comes ten seconds before night`, `night turns build mode off`, `the squad saw zombies in view` (four or more in one snapshot), `the driven player shot zombies through real input`, `the sentry fired at the horde`, a `note` of how many zombies the turrets killed (counted from `zkill` events with `by: null` in the page's frames; the squad often downs the small first wave before it comes in range), `by the low sentry the hint offers to reload it` and `holding E reloads the sentry` (with the scrap it took) (for the first turret whose ammo bar drops; a `note` when none does), a `note` with how low each turret's ammo bar went, and `a Dawn callout sums up the night`. ## Gotchas

- `doctor.sh` checks only the three public rooms. A squad exists only after a `POST /api/squads`.
- A squad with no human closes after 30s, bots and all, so open it and join within that window.
- `zombies.ts` needs no browser and launches none.
- The golden replay excludes zombies runs; `test/zombies-determinism.test.ts` guards their determinism instead.
- Bots revive fast, so the bled-out screen needs the fragile scratch copy and some luck; a run that logs `note revived before bleeding out` proves the downed UI but not the bled-out card.
- The core alert needs zombies to reach the core. On the stock night 1 a squad often stops them first, so `zom-core-alert.png` may be missing from the default run; the fragile copy always reaches it.
- Build mode has no touch control yet; on a phone B is out of reach.
- Stock day 1 affords one sentry after the build step's wall and never a cannon; the cannon path needs the more-scrap scratch copy.
- A bot near a sentry reloads it far faster than it fires, so its ammo bar may sit at 10/10 all night. The turret unit tests pin the tenths, and the browser run logs how low each bar went.
