# Joining and playing

Clicking a room and `Play` puts the player into a live match. A 4s banner states the objective and team once per round, and holding Tab shows a one-line objective under the score pill. WASD moves, the mouse aims through a canvas reticle, clicking fires, and closing the page removes the player from the room. Hits give the shooter a hitmarker and stacked damage numbers, and the victim a red arc on the side the damage came from.

## Sub-features

- `play-join` enters a room and swaps the menu for the HUD. The 17th human in a versus room gets `{t:'error', message:'Room full'}` and stays on the menu. The `welcome` frame carries the map's `worldSize` and walls.
- `play-standstill` freezes a room while no human is joined: no bots move and no clock runs. An observer `ws` client that sends `join` counts as a human and wakes it.
- `play-move` moves the player on the server at a base 255 units/s, scaled by armor and gun (about 237 with a pistol and light armor, so a 700ms `move` step covers about 166). The map edge is drawn as a raised curb.
- `play-input-queue` queues each client's inputs on the server, at most 8. When the backlog never drains during a 30-tick window, the server merges the oldest two, keeping any fire, reload, ability or use press and the higher shot count.
- `play-fire` fires and spends ammo on the server. A click during a reload is held and fires once the reload ends.
- `play-leave` removes the player from the room when the page unloads.
- `play-reconnect` rejoins on its own when the socket drops mid-match (a deploy restarts the server, or the network blips). The last frame stays on screen under a centered `#reconnect` card reading `Reconnecting… (attempt n)`. Retries back off from 0.5s to a 5s cap with jitter, and the browser's `online` event dials at once. The rejoin is a fresh join as a new player in the same room, with the same token and the current loadout, so a pick changed on the death screen sticks. Score and level reset. If the server still holds the old player (up to about 20s, until the heartbeat drops it), the name can get a number appended. Chat gains a `Reconnected.` line. After about 45s the client gives up, and the menu says `Lost connection. Press Play to try again.` Leaving the page never reconnects. Close code 1008 (a message flood or the 10s join timeout) goes to the menu instead of retrying. A `Room full` error during reconnect lands on the menu. A first connect that fails shows `Disconnected from server.`
- `play-objective` shows the `#objective` banner once per round, naming the mode, the player's team and the win score, then hides it after 4s. Joining, a new map, a new mode or team, and the round after a winner banner each show it; a respawn into the same round does not.
- `play-hit-feedback` turns each hit into one `dmg` event (attacker, victim, amount, kind `player` or `crate`) or an `impact` for walls. The shooter sees a hitmarker for player hits (larger and red on a kill) and floating damage numbers. Hits on one victim within 300ms merge into one number; a number that starts while others still float over the same victim starts 28 units above the highest one, so they never overlap. The victim sees a red arc around their player on the side of the attacker (the attacker's snapshot position, or the impact point when the attacker is out of view) for 600ms, plus a thin warm edge along the screen. Hits from one side refresh one arc. The muzzle flash sits at the barrel tip, each shot kicks the gun back, and a grey casing ejects to the gun's right, settles and fades. A round that strikes cover flashes small, leaves hairline cracks on the cover's top (at most 48 at once, each fading out over its last 3 of 10s, and gone with a broken crate) and knocks grey chips onto the floor beside it that settle and fade. Hits on bodies flash them white. Every gun round and its flash start at the muzzle the page draws. Yours leave your predicted body along your current aim the moment the page expects the server to fire them: the page steps your gun's trigger by the sim's rules (cooldown, bursts, auto fire, magazine, reload, the press grace) on every input it sends, so a click or a touch on the aim stick fires its round, flash, kick and sound at once, and a held trigger or a burst fires each shot between inputs when the gun is ready. Each own `shot` event then confirms the oldest drawn shot instead of drawing another; a server shot nobody drew is drawn when its event arrives, and a drawn shot the server is four inputs past without firing loses its rounds. After you let go the trigger stays held for an input or two while a drawn shot is still owed, so a server applying two inputs in one tick still fires it. Everyone else's leave their interpolated body along their drawn angle when the render clock reaches their shot. The page then skips the server's copies of those rounds, since lag compensation starts a human's a round trip plus the render delay down their line. Local rounds stop at the first wall, crate, zombie or enemy of the shooter on their line, past as many bodies as the gun pierces. Shrapnel, and rounds from shooters whose shot events never reached the page, come from snapshots.
- `play-reticle` replaces the OS cursor over the canvas (`#game.aiming` sets `cursor: none` while playing) with four ticks that sit where the gun's spread cone crosses the cursor distance. Grip or Choke narrows it. Holding a movement key widens it for an assault rifle (1.3x), an LMG (2x) or a sniper (4x). An assault rifle's ticks open further over a held spray after its third shot, up to double, and close within a quarter second of letting go, read from the predicted trigger's `heat`. While reloading, a small gold ring round the center dot fills over the reload, and the ticks step back to at least 12px so the ring never touches them. The spread comes from the sim's own `spreadFor` in `src/shared/sim/stats.ts`, so it matches the server exactly. The reticle shows only while aiming with the mouse, never in build mode.
- `play-hud-fade` keeps the score pill, leaderboard and minimap nearly opaque (97%, so cover under them never shows through) and eases each to 80% while any living player is drawn under it, so it stays a readable panel with the body just showing through. The `?dev` hook `skirmishDev.panels()` lists where each was last drawn, in CSS px.
- `play-look` follows `docs/art/gold-standard.webp`: a pale concrete floor with faint stains and grit under an 80px grid, and cover drawn top down, every wall, crate and building's top face exactly on its collision rect in a seeded material grain (sandstone blocks, concrete walls, planters for crates, slate engineer walls, brick squad walls, concrete pads under turrets), with a thin ink outline, a bright inner edge, a 4px dark lip down and right, and a long soft shadow cast down and to the right. The floor and the map's shadows are baked once per wall layout into one off-screen ground layer (`skirmishDev.shadowBakes()` counts the bakes, which hold still while you play); buildings re-blur it only when one goes up or comes down. Bodies are flat discs with an ink rim that armor thickens, a short ink gun and a soft drop shadow. Every visible body leaves a white dashed trail on the floor that fades within 0.9s; a dash leaves an unbroken streak. Rounds are short warm dashes in a soft glow. The HUD is minimal: health bar with `hp / max` and ammo top left, then a small line for the gun, its evolution and level, and one for the ability and perks; the score pill top center; the top five and your own place top right in a grey panel (while Tab is held, the whole board up to 12 rows, or 6 on a compact screen or under 760px tall) with the kill feed under it; the minimap bottom right.
- `play-team-colors` draws every TDM and DOM body in its team color, whatever color was picked, so the color alone tells teammates from enemies. Each joining human goes to the team with fewer humans (the smaller team on a tie), so two humans land on opposite sides. On every join and leave the bots are reseated so a human counts as three bots and the room keeps at least 18 players: a side whose humans all left gets three extra bots per human on the other side (at most 18 bots), and they leave again when a human joins it. FFA keeps the picked color, and an FFA enemy that wears your color gets a dashed red ring where your own player wears a thin ring in its own color.
- `play-tags` puts a small, low-contrast name under every other body, bots and humans, drawn beneath the bodies so a crowd hides a name rather than smearing it across a neighbor. Your own name never shows. Another body hit by anyone shows a thin health bar between it and its name for 1.8s, fading over the last 0.5s. Your own body shows the same bar whenever you are below full health and none at full. Nothing depends on hover, so touch play sees the same tags. `skirmishDev.tags()` lists the tags of the last frame (`id`, `bar`, `name`). The one other label is `KILLER · <name>` over your killer while you are dead.
- `play-names` gives each player a unique name in the room. A guest who types a registered name or a name already in use gets a number appended.
- `play-health` gives human players 400 HP and four times the regen (`HP_MULTIPLIER` in `src/shared/defs.ts`). Bots have 100 HP. Armor blocks the same share of each hit for both. A human's hits on another human deal four times the damage, then armor blocks its tier's share, so a human duel lasts as long as a bot duel in every armor tier. `duel.ts` shows this live: one pistol hit on a light-armored human is a `dmg` event of 99.4 (27 x 4, less light armor's 8%), drawn as `99`. Below full health your own body carries the slim bar from `play-tags`; at full it carries none.
- `play-view` sends each client only what lies inside its visible rectangle. The rectangle is the view radius across and radius divided by aspect tall, with the aspect clamped to 1..16/9, plus a 64-unit margin. The camera shows the same rectangle, and bots see the same one.
- `play-lag-comp` judges a shot against where targets were on the shooter's screen. The server pings each client every 2s and caps its rewind at the worst of its last five round trips plus the 100ms render delay and a 60ms margin, never more than 350ms (the full 350ms until the first pong). Time the input waited in the server's queue is added to the cap, still never past 350ms. Walls that stood during that window block it.
- `play-duel` two real browsers in one room see and damage each other.
- `play-muzzle` starts every human's drawn gun rounds at their drawn muzzle, on your own screen and on another lagged human's, and never draws the server's copy of those rounds while their shooter is in view.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Menu, click a room row, then `Play`.

## Driving it with drive.ts

Preconditions:

- Doctor passes.

- **Join.** Run `node drive.ts "$RUN" join`. Log lines `welcome frame received on the page socket`, `menu hidden and HUD shown`, `own player present in snapshots`, `server human count in ffa rises by one`, `observer leaderboard lists the player`. Screenshot `joined.png`.
- **Move.** Run `node drive.ts "$RUN" join move`. It holds `KeyD` for 700ms. Log line `holding D moves the player right on the server` with the measured x change. Screenshot `moved.png`.
- **Own-shot latency.** Run `LAG=<ms> node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/firefeel.ts "$RUN"` at 0, 40 and 80. It logs, per cue (`round`, `flash`, `kick`, `sound`), the median, p90 and max ms from the real mousedown (or the touch aim stick leaving its deadzone) to the page drawing or scheduling it, and per phase `counts: server shots N  sounds N  flashes N  kicks N  rejected predictions 0  drawn late 0`. Each check fails past 20ms median or 34ms p90, or when the counts differ. A phase in which a bot kills the driver or the round ends runs again. A second browser holds the SMG (`HOLDS=` sets how often, default 3), and the check fails when its mean gap between shots strays more than 10% from the SMG's `fireMs`.
- **Fire.** Run `node drive.ts "$RUN" join fire`. It clicks once, then taps six times just past the fire cooldown. Log lines `click fires: server ammo decreases` and `6 quick clicks fire 6 shots (server ammo)`, both with the measured ammo. Screenshot `fired.png`.
- **Latency.** A default step. Run `node drive.ts "$RUN" join latency`. Log line `own movement drawn within 50ms of keydown (median)` with the median, p95 and samples.
- **Reconnect.** Run `node drive.ts "$RUN" join reconnect leave` (or `account join reconnect`). Local runs only. `reconnect` needs the page in a match, so it must follow `join` with no `leave` or `restart` between: `restart` reloads the page to the menu, so `restart reconnect` and `leave … reconnect` fail by design. It restarts the server process on the same port and data dir. Log lines `page shows the reconnecting overlay over the game while the server is down` with the overlay text, `a new welcome arrives on the page socket without any click` with the old and new player ids and the delay, `HUD is back and the overlay is gone`, `observer on the restarted server lists the player`. Screenshots `reconnecting.png` and `reconnected.png`. The backoff, the 45s budget and which closes reconnect are covered by `test/client-reconnect.test.ts`.
- **Leave.** Run `node drive.ts "$RUN" join leave`. It reloads the page. Log line `the departed player drops off the observer leaderboard after the page unloads (reload)` on every run, and `server human count returns to baseline after the page unloads (reload)` on local runs only.
- **Objective, hits and team colors.** Run `node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/combat.ts "$RUN" tdm dom`. It writes `$RUN/evidence/combat.log` and ends in `RESULT PASS` or `RESULT FAIL`. Per room it logs `objective banner shows on join` with the banner text, `banner names the player's team from the snapshot`, `objective banner hides after about 4s`, and `a dmg event from the driven player arrives on the page socket` with the count and kinds. When a bot kills the driven player it respawns and logs `objective banner stays hidden after respawning into the same round`. Screenshots are `objective-<room>.png`, `muzzle-<room>.png`, `hit-crate-<room>.png`, and, when the chance comes up, `hit-player-<room>.png` (hitmarker), `hurt-<room>.png` (vignette) and `teammate-<room>.png` (team colors and marker).
- **Names.** Covered by `npm test` (`test/e2e.test.ts` and `test/names.test.ts`), which join real sockets as an impostor, the account owner and two guests named Alex.
- **Feedback cues.** Run `node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/gamefeel.ts "$RUN" [seconds]` (see [progression](./progression-death-modes.md)). It screenshots `hurt-arc.png`, `numbers-stack.png` (with `numbers floating over one victim keep apart` and the measured gap, read from `skirmishDev.liveNumbers()`), `reticle-spread.png`, `reticle-reload.png`, `names-crowd.png`, `self-bar.png` and `self-bar-hidden.png` (with `every other body in view is named and yours is not`, read from `skirmishDev.tags()`), `hud-fade.png` and `rival-ring.png` (loadout color `COLOR`, default red), and logs `the canvas hides the OS cursor while playing`.
- **Muzzle.** Run `LAG=80 JITTER=0 node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/muzzle.ts "$RUN" [seconds]`. Two lagged browsers walk the map's nav paths (`navGrid` and `findPath` from `src/server/bot/nav.ts`) toward each other until within 380px, logging `the two browsers met within 380px before shooting`; if they have not met within 45s it logs `FAIL` and `RESULT FAIL` and exits. Then both strafe and tap the pistol, and it logs the own-round and other-human medians (each must be at most 25px over at least 5 rounds) and that no server copy of either human's rounds was drawn with its shooter in view. Log `$RUN/evidence/muzzle.log`.
- **Duel.** Run `node duel.ts "$RUN"`. Both browsers walk the map's nav paths toward each other, rebuilt whenever a `welcome` or `walls` frame brings a new map, with the room's crates stamped in, so 350-500px walls on a 6000px map no longer trap them. The hunter fires only when the target is within 420px, in its snapshots and in clear line of sight past walls and crates (`clearShot` from the same module). The hunt has 180s. Log lines `both browsers joined the same room`, `each browser had the other in its own snapshots`, `hunter's socket shows a dmg event naming the target` and `target's socket shows the same hit from the hunter`. Screenshots `duel-hunter-view.png` and `duel-target-view.png`.

## Gotchas

- Spawning against a wall can block movement. A move under 50 units fails the check. Retry once before calling it a regression.
- The pistol is semi-automatic. A held mouse fires once. Press and release for each shot. One press made during the cooldown is held and fires the moment the gun is ready; extra presses in the same cooldown add nothing. A held automatic fires at its own rate (an LMG's 90ms, not the next 33ms tick).
- TDM and DOM rooms are reached by clicking the 2nd or 3rd `#servers .server`. `drive.ts` joins FFA only. `combat.ts` joins any room.
- `#game` has `cursor: none` while playing. A CDP screenshot never shows the OS cursor anyway, so the reticle in screenshots is the canvas one.
- The hitmarker lasts 220ms, so `hit-player-<room>.png` exists only when a bot came within range and the screenshot landed in time. A crate hit alone still passes the dmg check, and the log says the hitmarker was not exercised.
- After navigating to another room, the old page can stay in the back/forward cache with its socket open. `combat.ts` reads frames only from the newest socket.
- `scripts/measure-lag-aim.ts` (outside this skill) fails with `no clear arena` on the hand-designed maps. The rewind-specific hit-rate measurement is unavailable until it is fixed. Hits landing in `duel` and `fire` are the live evidence for lag compensation meanwhile.
- A map change teleports every living player to a fresh spawn, so a move check that spans one can fail. See [maps](./maps.md).
