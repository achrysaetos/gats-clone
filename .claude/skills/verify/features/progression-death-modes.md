# Progression, death and modes

Score in one life climbs the level ladder (`LEVELS` in `src/shared/defs.ts`): a tier 1 perk at 100, a gun evolution at 200, a tier 2 perk at 300, an ability on Space at 400, and a second evolution at 550. Each evolution offers the two branches of the current gun (6 class guns, 12 stage-1, 24 stage-2). A stage-2 gun makes its holder hunted. Dying resets score, perks and gun to the class gun and opens a death card docked left under the minimap: the killer and their gun, what the life had earned, a respawn countdown and a loadout re-pick, while the killer wears a red ring in the world. Every mode ends rounds with a winner banner. TDM and DOM add team scores.

## Sub-features

- `prog-perks` docks a row of perk tiles (key, symbol, short name) at the bottom center, between the chat and the minimap, at each perk level. A line under the dock title (`#perk-panel .perk-desc`) names the hovered or focused choice and says what it does; at 1700px and wider every perk tile also prints its description under its name. A number key or a click picks, and the pick plays a short two-note confirm cue once the server applies it. The client sends `{t:'pick', level, option}`; the server ignores a pick for any level but the pending one. No pick is offered or accepted during the round-end ceasefire, since the restart wipes it, so the dock stays closed under the winner banner.
- `prog-evolve` uses the same dock at each evolve level with two wide tiles (gun art in the branch's accent color, name, one-line description), titled `Level up · evolve your <gun>`. Picking swaps the gun and keeps the loaded share of the magazine (half a pistol magazine becomes half a Hand Cannon magazine), and a reload in progress continues. A max-health perk scales current health by the new maximum over the old, so a pick is never a free heal. The line under the ammo count names the gun in its accent color with one accent pip per stage. The evolution itself is announced: a ring in the new gun's accent color bursts around your player, the gun name and description show centered in the upper third for 2.4s, and a rising `evolve` cue plays.
- `prog-tier-mark` draws chevrons over every evolved player: one silver at stage 1, two gold at stage 2. Evolved guns are longer or thicker, may have two or three barrels, carry an accent stripe, and fire tracers in their own color and size. Each gun has its own shot sound.
- `prog-hunted` marks a stage-2 enemy with a thin pulsing red ring with four ticks in the world and a red diamond on every enemy minimap at any distance. The diamond stays inside the minimap border even for a player at the map edge. Each hunted ping outside your view also gets a pulsing red chevron clamped to the screen edge along its bearing from your player. Reaching stage 2 yourself shows a `You are HUNTED` callout with a line on the minimap ping and the bounty, and a red `HUNTED` badge beside your health numbers while you hold the gun; your own player gets no reticle. The diamond is a ping: it holds where the last ping caught them, pings come every 2.5s and on every unsilenced shot, and a fading ring marks each ping. A silenced hunted gun (Phantom, Specter, Ghost) shows only on the 2.5s pings. Snapshot minimap marks carry `pingAge` (null for a live mark). Teammates never get the reticle or the hunted minimap marker. The `hunted` event ships on the tick after the pick. The kill feed shows `<name> is hunted` with a target icon, and a kill of a hunted player shows a `+200 BOUNTY` tag and pays 200 extra points. The kill feed names an evolved gun in its accent color; class guns stay icons. When you make the bounty kill, a gold `BOUNTY +200` callout and a `bounty` cue replace the plain kill confirm.
- `prog-catch-up` multiplies score by 1.5 while your level is below the average level of the other living players. The only on-screen sign is the kill popup: each of your kills floats `+N` at the victim, where N is your snapshot score delta, so a catch-up kill reads `+150`.
- `prog-ability` uses the ability (the tier 3 perk) with Space, then puts it on cooldown. An ability that cannot be used keeps its cooldown ready: an engineer wall that would overlap a player is skipped until the spot clears.
- `prog-dash` bursts 240px over 200ms along the held movement keys, or along the aim when no key is held. The client predicts it, so the local player does not snap back. A dashing body leaves a fading trail.
- `prog-knife` lunges up to 90px along the aim, stopping at walls, crates and the map edge, and strikes the first enemy in reach (50 damage, ignores armor, 4s cooldown), including one at point blank. Every use draws a short sweeping arc at the strike point and plays a slash sound.
- `prog-other-abilities` covers grenade, frag, gas and mine. They exist, but no step drives them. An owner keeps at most two mines (a third replaces the oldest), and they vanish when the owner dies.
- `combat-blast` explosions (blast rounds, grenades, mines) stop at walls: a body or crate whose line from the blast center crosses a wall takes nothing, and crates take the same distance falloff as players. A blast deals its owner half damage; the owner's bullets, shrapnel and gas never hurt them, and teammates are always spared, even after the owner leaves. A player finished by their own blast gives the kill, score, bounty and team point to whoever dealt them the most damage in the last 10 seconds; with no such attacker it credits nothing.
- `combat-shield` blocks 33% of bullet damage within 40 degrees of the facing; blasts, knives and gas go through it. The other tier-2 picks are thick skin (+40 max health) and first aid (regen 3x as fast, starting 1.6s after a hit instead of 4s). `node scripts/bench-balance.ts 0` prints perk duel tables for three scenarios: shield wins head-on, thick skin wins after a burst of unblockable damage, and first aid wins a second fight 5s after the first.
- `combat-human-damage` scales a human's hits by the victim's health multiplier before armor absorbs its share: 3x against a human, 1x against a bot. Human armor points are tripled with health. Human duels last as long as bot duels in every armor tier; bots deal base damage, so they spend triple the damage on a human.
- `combat-assist` pays 50 points to every other living attacker who dealt at least 30% of the victim's max health. The kill event lists them in `assisters`, and the assister sees an outlined gold `+50 assist` above the crosshair.
- `prog-ring` reads `Ability at 400` where the ability will sit, and `Pick an ability` while the ability pick is open. Once picked, the ability shows its icon in a ring that fills as it cools down, with `SPACE` when ready.
- `round-reset` returns every player to level 0 with no perks and the class gun when a round restarts in any mode, along with score, kills and deaths. Every living player's life so far is credited to their account, and they get a fresh life (full health, armor and ammo). Team scores and zones reset too, and the map advances to the next in the rotation.
- `round-ceasefire` holds the 8s end-of-round window as a ceasefire. Nobody can fire or use abilities, and bullets, grenades and gas already in flight deal no player damage.
- `round-winner` shows the banner `<winner> wins the round` (a team in TDM and DOM, a player name in FFA), a reason line when the ranking alone does not explain the win (`<name> reached 20 kills` for an FFA human behind a bot, `Time ran out` for a TDM time-limit win), the top three and `Next round in Ns`. The FFA podium puts the winner first, then ranks by round kills and fewer deaths. The objective banner is hidden while a winner is shown. A timed round with nobody to crown (an FFA round with no kills, a TDM round level on score and kills) shows no banner and restarts at once on the next map.
- `death-screen` docks `#death` top left, where the vitals sit while you live, so the world center stays visible. `#death-title` names the killer, `#death-cause` the killer's weapon (and, after a hunted death, the bounty they took), `#death-lost` the level, evolved gun and perks the life had earned. The card ignores pointer input (`#death[inert]`, card at 70% opacity) for 700ms after it opens, so a trigger click still in flight cannot repick the loadout. `#respawn` is disabled during the countdown. While you are dead the killer wears a red ring and a `KILLER · <name>` label in the world.
- `death-respawn` returns the player with the new loadout. The objective banner does not come back for a respawn into the same round.
- `mode-tdm` and `mode-dom` show team scores, the objective under the score bar, and the winner banner at the win score. TDM wins at 50 kills, or after 10 minutes goes to the team ahead on score, then on kills. DOM wins at 1000 points, earning 5 points per second per held zone. A lone team on a zone first drains another team's partial capture, so an owner standing on its point pushes an attack back. A held zone needs one full capture (3s) to turn neutral and another to be taken.
- `mode-ffa` ends the round early when a human reaches 20 kills; a bot at 20 does not end it. Otherwise the 6-minute map timer ends the round and the player with the most kills wins, bot or human, fewer deaths breaking a tie. The leaderboard shows round kills under `Most kills · m:ss left`, and the objective reads `most kills · m:ss left · first player to 20 ends it`; in TDM it reads `first to 50 kills · m:ss left`. The clock counts down to `match.roundEndsAt`, a server time that stays fixed for the round (null in DOM and during a round end); before the first snapshot the text falls back to the full length (`most kills in 6:00`). Every mode's leaderboard ranks by round kills, then fewer deaths, and keeps an FFA winner on the board during the banner.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Earn points by destroying crates and killing players.
- Get killed by a bot or another player.
- Join the TDM or DOM room from the menu.

## Gotchas

- Score, level, perks and the gun reset on respawn and on a round restart.
- The engineer wall, the knife's wall stop and the round reset are covered by `npm test` (`test/abilities.test.ts`, `test/progression.test.ts`) rather than the browser driver.
- Gameplay rules are covered by `npm test` (`test/sim.test.ts`). The map here is about what the player sees.
- Do not lower the TDM or DOM win score in the scratch copy used to drive `abilities.ts`. Rounds then end every few seconds, the restart resets level and perks, and `reached tier 3 and picked …` fails. Lower only the `LEVELS` scores for abilities.
- Bots climb the same ladder. In a room of bots about 35% of lives reach the first evolution, 12% the ability and 5% a hunted gun, so a few minutes of watching shows bot abilities and a hunted bot. `node scripts/bench-balance.ts` measures it, and `test/balance.test.ts` pins a fixed-seed room.
- Catch-up makes a kill worth 150 while you trail the room's average level, so the first kill can skip past 100. The dock still opens at the lowest open pick.
- With `LEVELS` at 0..50 a life climbs to stage 2 in seconds, so `evolve-stage2.png` often stacks the stage-1 and stage-2 callouts, and nearly every bot is hunted, which crowds the screen edge with chevrons. At stock thresholds evolutions are minutes apart.
- `gamefeel.ts` once saw a real click on an enabled `#respawn` leave the player dead for 3s; the next click worked. The audit run before this UI pass shows the same pattern (a second death screen with the same killer 6s after the first), so it predates the docked card. On a repeat, the log line carries the button state, the element under the click, the respawn frames the page sent and the snapshot's `respawnIn`.
