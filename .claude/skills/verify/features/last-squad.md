# Last Squad

Last Squad is the `br` room: a battle royale for six squads of three, one squad per color, on the four versus maps in rotation. A shrinking ring closes in six phases (`RING` in `src/shared/defs.ts`) and burns anyone outside it through armor and the spawn shield. A player whose health runs out while a squadmate stands is knocked: they crawl with half their max health as a pool enemies can shoot through to finish them, and a squadmate holding E within 70px for 3 seconds revives them. Squads start evenly spaced 2200 px from the map's centre, facing in, with four caches worth 100 at the centre and 100 extra crates scattered on open ground (50 within 1500 px of the centre, 25 farther out), plus 100 more inside each new circle. While the first three phases run nobody is out: the dead redeploy beside a standing squadmate after 15 seconds, and a squad wiped whole regroups together on the edge 15 seconds later. Once the third phase closes, `Last lives`: whoever falls stays down and a squad with nobody standing is out. A supply drop per phase lands inside the next circle and jumps its breaker to their next level pick. The last squad standing wins. Dead players spectate a squadmate, their killer or a survivor, and a squad that is out gets its result card.

## Sub-features

- `br-room` is listed by `/api/servers` as `{ id: 'br', mode: 'BR' }`. `createRoom` fills it to six squads of three with bots. A joiner while redeploys are open takes the place of a bot in the squad with fewest humans (`seatFor` and `takeSeat` in `src/shared/sim/royale.ts`): its spot and its state, up, knocked or waiting to redeploy. A leaver hands the place back to a new bot. After the third phase closes a joiner gets `team: null` and a dead life until the next match's `startRoyale` seats them.
- `br-ring` is `Royale.ring` in `src/shared/sim/world.ts`, a state machine of `waiting`, `shrinking` and `closed`. The snapshot's `royale.ring` is `{ phase, from, to, shrinkAt, closeAt }`, and `ringAt(ring, serverTime)` in `src/shared/protocol.ts` gives the circle at any time on both server and client. `phase` counts the phases closed. Ring damage ships one `dmg` event a second with `attacker: null`.
- `br-knock` turns a death into `life: 'downed'` while a squadmate is up. The `kill` event carries `knock: true` and pays the kill. A finish ships `life` with `k: 'finished'` and `by` the finisher, or `by: null` for the ring. Revives ship `revived`, bleed-outs `bledOut`, redeploys and regroups `redeployed`, and a squad with nobody standing ships `{ e: 'wiped', team, place }`, with `place: null` when it regroups instead of going out.
- `br-lives` is the `lives` column of `RING` (`'many'` for the first three rows, `'last'` after). `redeploysOpen` reads the row of the phase in progress. A squad wiped while it is open goes into `Royale.regroupAt` instead of `out`; `redeploy` brings the whole squad back at that time through `spawnPoint`, which in BR puts a squad with nobody standing on `farthestEdgeSlot`: the starting bearing farthest from the squads still up, on a circle `min(2200, r - 220)` round the next circle's centre. Comebacks already pending when lives turn last still happen.
- `br-loot` is `Crate.tier` (`loot`, `rich`, `cache`, `drop`), with score, health and size in `CRATE_TIERS` in `src/shared/defs.ts`. `stockCrates` in `src/shared/sim/royale.ts` tiers the map's own crates `loot`, places the caches and scatters 100 crates; `advanceRing` scatters `ROYALE.wave` more inside each new circle. `CrateView.tier` reaches the client, which draws caches as brick and frames rich crates and caches in gold (`drawLootWorld` in `src/client/royale.ts`).
- `br-view` is `snap.royale`: `ring`, `redeploys` (false once the third phase closes), `squads` (`{ team, pips: ('up' | 'down' | 'dead')[], place, regroupAt }`), `redeployAt` (server time you come back, alone or with your regrouping squad, or null), `drops` (`{ x, y, landsAt }`, 0 once landed and still standing), `watch` (the player id the camera follows while dead) and `result` (`{ place, of, kills, knocks, revives }` once your squad is out or the match is over). It is sticky on the wire.
- `br-drops` announce 10 seconds before landing, 20 seconds into each phase's wait. A landed drop is a crate with `tier: 'drop'` and 300 hp. Crates never respawn.
- `br-hud` is the pill (`RING 2/6` and the time to the ring's next move, `FINAL` at the end), the squad tracker under it (six columns of three pips: filled up, hollow knocked, faint dead, `12s` in the squad's color while it regroups, `#n` once out), the storm tint and the edge outside the circle with the next circle dashed in the world and on the minimap, `Ring closes in 0:24` or `Ring closing · 0:12` above the minimap with `Last lives` under it once redeploys close, gold drop marks, `KNOCKED` tags and finish, redeploy and `Red squad is out · #4` and `Red squad wiped · regrouping` lines in the feed, the downed banner, `Hold E to revive <name>`, `Watching <name>` with the redeploy countdown, `Squad wiped · regrouping in 0:12`, or the squad's place while dead, `The ring is moving` and `Last lives` callouts, the `knock` and `ring` sounds, and `#report` with `#1 · Last squad standing` or `#4 of 6` and kills, knocks and revives. The death card stays closed in this mode.
- `br-bots` are `src/server/bot/royale.ts`. `packFor` picks the squad's goal in one place: in from the ring, a knocked squadmate, back to the squad, trailing the lowest-id squadmate, then for the leader the best crate by points over distance plus 400 px when the squad's mean score is behind, or the nearest lead within 2000 px when level or ahead; from the ring closing on last lives it is `hold`, cover by the squad's middle. `strays` lets the versus brain's fights, searches and flanks run and replaces only idling or wandering off. A knocked bot crawls toward a squadmate.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- On the menu pick the room marked `BR` and press Play. Run in toward the brick caches at the centre, shooting gold-framed crates on the way. Stay inside the circle, hold E beside a knocked squadmate, and shoot the gold-framed drop when it lands.

## Driving it with drive.ts

Preconditions:

- Doctor passes and lists four rooms.

- **The client in a browser.** The stock ring takes six minutes, so launch a scratch copy with a fast, hard ring, never by editing the repo: `rsync -a --exclude node_modules --exclude .git <repo>/ "$SCR/repo/"`, `ln -s <repo-or-parent>/node_modules "$SCR/repo/node_modules"`, then in `$SCR/repo/src/shared/defs.ts` set every `RING` row's `waitMs` to `10_000` (the first to `12_000`), every `shrinkMs` to `6_000`, `dps` to `0.06` for the first three rows, every row's `lives` to `'last'` except the first, and `ROYALE.dropLandMs` to `8_000`. Run `$SCR/repo/.claude/skills/verify/scripts/launch.sh "$SCR/run"` and `node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/royale-ui.ts "$SCR/run"` from `$SCR/repo`. It joins through the menu (`room: 3`), runs along the nav grid toward the centre until a cache is on screen, then walks straight away from the next circle's centre and stays out. It logs `the menu joins the Last Squad room`, `the player is seated in a squad of 3`, `six squads are tracked`, `the squad starts out on the edge, about 2200 px from the centre`, `running in, the caches at the centre come into view among rich crates`, `the ring catches the player outside it`, `the ring hurts the player outside it` (counting `dmg` events with no attacker), `the ring takes the player down`, `a knocked player stays in play, not on the death screen`, `the player dies`, `a dead player watches someone`, `the camera follows the watched player` (from `skirmishDev.toScreen`, within 120px of the centre), `the death screen stays closed while spectating`, `the result card shows the place`, `it reads as a place with kills, knocks and revives` and `the feed carried knocks and squad wipes`. Screenshots: `br-start`, `br-loot`, `br-ring`, `br-knocked`, `br-spectate`, `br-result`. Log: `$SCR/run/evidence/royale-ui.log`. About 2 minutes.
- **A regroup in a browser.** In a second scratch copy keep the fast ring but set `lives: 'many'` on the first five rows and `ROYALE.squadSize` to `1`, so the ring wipes the player's one-player squad while lives are many. Run `node .claude/skills/verify/scripts/royale-ui.ts "$SCR/run" regroup`. After the death it logs `the feed says the squad was wiped and regroups`, `the spectate line counts down the regroup` and `the tracker column counts it down too` (both read from `skirmishDev.royale()`), `the squad comes back`, `together on the edge of the next circle` and `the tracker shows the squad up again`. Screenshots: `br-regroup`, `br-regrouped`.
- **The rules without a browser.** `node --test test/royale.test.ts` covers the ring, knocks, finishes, squads going out, placement, friendly fire, redeploys, regroups and last lives, edge starts, caches, scattered crates and crate waves, spectating, drops, crates and seats.
- **Pacing.** `node scripts/bench-royale.ts --seeds 32` plays 128 bot matches and prints match length, ring deaths, regroups, fights a minute by phase, the last fight's phase and the proxy squad's wins, then for every 30 seconds the share of bot time standing still, speed, shots a bot-minute, crates broken, knocks and kills, the mean score of those standing and the median distance to the nearest enemy.

## Gotchas

- A room stands still until a human joins, so the ring's clock starts with the first joiner.
- A knocked bot crawls toward its nearest standing squadmate, so `downed` players move.
- A dead player who is still waiting to redeploy also spectates; the label then counts down to the redeploy.
- With the stock ring the first three phases never put a squad out, so a `wiped` event with a place first shows after the third phase closes, around 3 minutes in. The `regroup` run needs its own scratch copy.
- A crate wave lands inside each new circle the moment it is drawn, so the crate count jumps by 100 at each phase change.
